# reads it again, vr_humlook_info prints what the nearest armed humanoid wears. The Texture Studio (Debug > Tools) writes it.
#
# A row: <type> <category> <part> r g b strength light tint
#   type      grunt, enforcer, knight, dknight (the armed monsters' rows, QC VR_AM_*)
#   category  explosive (rockets, grenades), energy (lightning, plasma, lasers), flame, poison; the others (shells, nails,
#             melee, the monster's own gun) have no row: as today; elite: an elite's clothes whatever it holds (its pack
#             keeps its weapon's colour, else the elite pack row; QC vr_attrs.qc), its lightness pulsing (vr_elite_pulse)
#   part      clothes (its body's masked texels), pack (its worn backpack, and a pack in its body's skin)
# Which texels: humlook_masks.txt (their palette rows; a few painted by hand). Faces and flesh are never changed.
#   r g b     the colour (0..1) the texels are dyed: each keeps its own lightness (its shading), in that colour
#   strength  how much of the dye (0 none, 1 all)
#   light     the texels' lightness times this
#   tint      0: the dye (the colour at the texel's lightness); 1: the texel's own colour times the colour (keeps more
#             of its detail, less of the colour); between: a mix
#
