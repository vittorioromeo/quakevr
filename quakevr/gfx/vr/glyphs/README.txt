Controller button glyphs (gfx/vr/glyphs)
=========================================

Source
------
The files named quest_*, index_*, frame_*, xbox_* and switch_* are from Kenney's "Input Prompts" pack (version 1.5A),
by Kenney (www.kenney.nl). Our copy came from the pack's mirror on OpenGameArt (opengameart.org), because kenney.nl's
own download page refused automated downloads when the pack was fetched (2026-11-28).

No attribution is required; we credit Kenney anyway. The pack's own license text is LICENSE.txt in this folder.

Our own glyphs
--------------
The files named drawn_* (PlayStation, the generic gamepad, Vive and WMR buttons) are Quake VR's own drawings, made in
the same style by Misc/quakevr/make_controller_art.py. They are not part of Kenney's pack.

Used by
-------
The Controller Bindings screen (Quake/vr/vr_bindsetup.cpp) and the bindings reminder panels above the weapon wheel and
in the main menu (Quake/vr/vr_bindpanel.cpp). Which glyph each button uses: Quake/vr/vr_bindsetup_art.inc (generated by
make_controller_art.py).
