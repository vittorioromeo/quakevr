hint Eight armed humanoids with guns in two loose rows, the back row behind the front one. With a friend in their line of fire they hold and sidestep for a clear shot instead of shooting through it (Combat > Armed Grunts: Careful of Friends, Gives Up After); one stepping in mid-burst can still be hit now and then. Set Careful of Friends to 0 to see the old chaos.
