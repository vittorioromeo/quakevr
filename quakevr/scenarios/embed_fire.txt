hint Right hand: the Crystal Lance. Hold the trigger to charge it, stab an ogre with its tip and let go once it is full: the bolt fires from the stuck Lance's tip, through the ogre. Or stab first, keep your grip on it, then charge and let go. Pull it out while charging: the charge goes on in your hand. Open your hand on it: its trigger does nothing. Flat-screen: hold fire then V, or V then hold fire; a tap of fire pulls it instead.
