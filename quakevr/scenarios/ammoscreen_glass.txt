title Ammo screens as deep glass: shotgun and super shotgun in hand
map vrtesthall
god
notarget
give "Shotgun" main
give "Super Shotgun" off
cmd impulse 9
hint Look down at the shotgun's end face (flat screen: about 75 degrees down): dark grey-green glass in a thick black frame, rounded corners, an inset shadow under the top edge, pale glowing digits, a faint sheen, no green outline. Screens > Rounded Glass Ammo Screens off brings back the old one. Check the SSG, and the nail pistol from the toolgun or impulse 9.
