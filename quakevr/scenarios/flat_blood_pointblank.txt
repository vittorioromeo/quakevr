title Blood at point blank: guns, grunts close by
hint Play with -flatscreenmode (or the headset). Kill one grunt and walk right up to its ragdoll; walk right up to a live one too. Shoot each from as close as you can with the shotgun, then the super shotgun (2), then the nailgun: each shot bleeds as much as from a few steps back. Particle Fade Near Eyes and Particles Hidden Nearer are 0 here.
