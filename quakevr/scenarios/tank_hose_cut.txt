# tank_hose_cut: see docs/vr-port/TESTING.md, "Checklist scenarios: how to write one" (needs Arcane Dimensions)
title A flamethrower and a poison sprayer lying with their tanks, grunts near, a sword in hand: cut the hoses
map vrtesthall
god
give "Knight's Sword" main
spawn "AD Flamethrower" 120 60
spawn "AD Poison Sprayer" 120 -60
spawn "Grunt" 320 90
spawn "Grunt" 320 -90
hint Slash the sword across a hose lying between a gun and its tank: a hiss and a snap, sparks and a puff; both keep a short stub. The gun is spent; force grab the tank, throw it at the grunts and shoot it: it bursts with the fuel it kept. A slow wave or a punch doesn't cut.
