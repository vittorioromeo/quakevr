# flat_embed_dual: see docs/vr-port/TESTING.md, "Checklist scenarios: how to write one". Flat-screen (-flatscreenmode): dual wielding a blade and a gun, the blade stuck in a grunt keeps its hand (axestick::handOnBlade, flatplay::holdsThing; vr_embed_test 52). ROUND25.md, "embeddual".
title Flat-screen: an axe stuck in a grunt keeps its hand, dual wielding a shotgun
hint Play with -flatscreenmode. Swing the axe (left mouse button) into a grunt until it stays in: the right hand stays on the axe's handle, the shotgun stays one-handed, and the right mouse button fires it (no ironsight). Walk back until it comes out (or the hand lets go of it out of reach): the usual pose returns, and with the hand empty the free hand goes onto the shotgun and the ironsight works again.
