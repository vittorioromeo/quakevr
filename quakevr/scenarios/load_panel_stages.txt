# load_panel_stages: see docs/vr-port/TESTING.md, "Checklist scenarios: how to write one". The hub loaded with the
# loading panel shown from the load's start (vr_load_panel_shot: normally only past a load's first second), so its
# stages show even when the caches make it quick (flatloadbar, ROUND26.md). Its images are saved too
# (screenshots/load_panel_entities.png, ..._models, ..._sounds, ..._lightgrid when the model lighting is built).
title Loading panel: the map load's stages over a bar
map vrstart
cvar vr_load_panel 1
cvar vr_load_panel_shot 0.5
hint While it loads, the panel names each slow part ("Spawning the map's monsters and items: 91 of 181 entities", "Lighting the models", "Loading sounds") over a moving bar, in the window and the headset. In the window it is small and centred over the last screen (console, menu) dimmed by half.
