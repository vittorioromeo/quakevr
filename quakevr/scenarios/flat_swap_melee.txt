# flat_swap_melee: see docs/vr-port/TESTING.md, "Checklist scenarios: how to write one". Flat-screen (-flatscreenmode) hand swap (3) with a melee weapon held alone (vr_flatswap.cpp; vr_drawblend.cpp; QC vr_flatswap_test 3).
title Flat-screen hand swap with melee weapons: the axe and the others stay in sight
hint Play with -flatscreenmode. With the axe in your right hand and the left empty, press 3: the left hand lets go to the front, the axe is brought across and changes hands there, and both hands and the axe stay in view all the way into the new hold (they used to vanish under the view for a moment). Press 3 again to swap back. Try it with the two-handed stance off too (Locomotion > Desktop Screen Play, vr_flat_melee_2h 0), and with the sword, crowbar and chainsaw lying here.
