# embed_stab: see docs/vr-port/TESTING.md, "Checklist scenarios: how to write one". Held stabs and swung axes stuck in bodies.
title Embedded blades: stab with the sword, swing the axe at the grunts, one in plate
map vrtesthall
god
notarget
give "Knight's Sword" main
give "Axe" off
spawn "Grunt" 120 -60
spawn "Grunt" 120 60
spawn "Armed Grunt: Axe" 200 0
spawn "Shambler" 320 0
hint Stab hard, tip first: the sword stays in. Slide it in and out, pull it free, or open your hand and grip it again. Swing the axe hard, edge first. A strong stab staggers a grunt: pull or push the blade to move it; walk away holding it to drag it, slowly; past arm's reach it comes out. The shambler behind them is too heavy: no stagger, no drag. Reset to try again.
