title Sliding in VR (experimental): run down the hall's long stretch and pull the turning stick straight down
map vrtesthall
start 0 -288 25 90
god
notarget
cvar vr_slide 1
hint Sliding (VR) is on for this test. Run ahead, then pull the turning stick firmly straight down: you slide, faster at first, slowing to a stop about 400 units on (let go to stop sooner; jump to keep the speed). Snap or smooth turns, or a pull well off straight down, never slide. Sliding, your view dips quickly to the desktop screen's sliding eye height and comes back up after (Slide View).
