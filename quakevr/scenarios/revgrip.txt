hint Press B (or Y) with the dagger or sword: it flips in your hand, blade down, with a click; again, back up. Toss one, let it turn over in the air and catch it blade down: you hold it reversed. Stab the grunt from above, blade down. A force grab always takes a blade upright; holstered and drawn, it keeps its grip.
