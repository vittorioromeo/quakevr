# speed_toggle_click: see docs/vr-port/TESTING.md, "Checklist scenarios: how to write one". The speed toggle by the
# stick click, debounced (vr_stick_click_debounce), and the double tap the Default Speed's other (vr_move_doubletap).
title Speed toggle by stick click, double tap for the other speed
map vrtesthall
start 0 -288 25 90
god
notarget
cvar vr_sprint_toggle 1
cvar vr_move_doubletap 1
hint Walk or run down the hall and click the moving stick once at a time, many times: each click switches the speed once, never twice. Then double-tap the stick (push, let go, push again): you get the other speed than your Default Speed (walking if it is Run) until you let go.
