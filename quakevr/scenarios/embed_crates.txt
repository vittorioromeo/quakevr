# embed_crates: see docs/vr-port/TESTING.md, "Checklist scenarios: how to write one". Blades stuck in wooden crates and barrels (his notes 2026-11-08, items 13 and 14; vr_embed_test 30).
title Embedded blades in crates and barrels: stab with the sword, swing the axe
cvar vr_embed 1
cvar vr_embed_props 1
map vrtesthall
god
notarget
give "Knight's Sword" main
give "Axe" off
spawn "Wooden Crate" 70 -40
spawn "Large Crate" 80 30
spawn "Barrel" 70 90
spawn "Barrel" 130 0
hint Stab a crate hard, tip first, or swing the axe hard into a barrel, edge first: the blade stays in. Holding it, move your hand aside: the crate drags along, heavily; lift: one hand only tips it, both hands lift it. Open your hand: the blade stays in, and rides the crate when you push it. Grip it again and pull it straight back: it slides out, harder than out of a body. Break the crate with a blade in it: the blade falls free (held: back in your hand). Reset to try again.
