# flat_prone_dual: see docs/vr-port/TESTING.md, "Checklist scenarios: how to write one". Flat-screen (-flatscreenmode): lying down with a gun in each hand, on vrslopes' 10-degree slope facing up it (the spot of vr_flatpronedual_test's slope run, Quake/vr/vr_flatmove.cpp).
title Flat-screen: lying down, dual wielding
hint Play with -flatscreenmode. You lie on a gentle slope with a shotgun in each hand: look level, a little up and a little down and fire both (LMB, RMB); then crawl back off the block onto the flat floor and try again. The guns sit clear of the ground at your sides, aim at the crosshair and fire; only looking well down do they lie on the floor and stop.
