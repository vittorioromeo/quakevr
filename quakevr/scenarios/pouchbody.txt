# (ROUND25.md, "pouchbody"; his note 2026-11-21: with vr_body_mode 0 the ammo pouch couldn't be used): Body Type Only
# Hands for the test, the shotgun in the main hand and the nailgun in the off hand, shells, nails and rockets in the
# reserve (the grenade pouch at the small of the back gives grenades while there are rockets).
title Pouches and holsters with Body Type: Only Hands
map vrtesthall
god
notarget
cvar vr_body_mode 0
give "Shotgun" main
give "Nailgun" off
cmd give s 20
cmd give n 50
cmd give r 10
hint Body Type is Only Hands for this test. Take a shell from the ammo pouch on the front of your belt and load the shotgun; drop the nailgun's magazine (B/Y) and seat one from the pouch; take a grenade from the pouch at the small of your back; put a gun in each hip and chest holster: all where they are with the body drawn.
