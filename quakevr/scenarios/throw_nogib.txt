# throw_nogib: see docs/vr-port/TESTING.md, "Checklist scenarios: how to write one". A running throw blade first kills whole.
title Thrown blades don't gib: a sword and a dagger to throw running at a row of grunts
cvar vr_embed 1
map vrtesthall
god
notarget
give "Knight's Sword" main
give "Dagger" off
spawn "Grunt" 400 -80
spawn "Grunt" 400 0
spawn "Grunt" 400 80
hint Run at the grunts and throw the sword and the dagger point first, hard: each dies whole with the blade deep in its body (about 80% of the blade in), no gibs. A handle-first or flat throw is as before; with Quad a point-first throw still gibs.
