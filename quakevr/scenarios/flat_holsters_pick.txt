title Flat holsters: let go to pick, DROP BOTH, H's order
map vrtesthall
cvar vr_flat_holsters 1

hint Hold the mouse wheel's button, point at a holster's L or R and let go: that button is used. Let go over no button: the menu just closes. DROP BOTH (under the hands, in the middle) drops both guns, and is dim with both hands empty. Out of the menu, H drops the left hand's gun first, then the right's.
