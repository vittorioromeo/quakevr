hint A grunt and an enforcer replaced by armed ones (Combat > Armed Grunts > Armed Humanoid Replacements: Gun and Melee Weapon 1): each holds its own gun in its right hand and an axe or a dagger in its left (by the Axe and Dagger weights). They fire the gun at range, take to the blade when you walk up to them, and go back to the gun when you back off. Their guns hurt as much as the vanilla ones.
