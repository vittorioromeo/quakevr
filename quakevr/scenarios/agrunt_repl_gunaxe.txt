hint A grunt and an enforcer replaced by armed ones (Combat > Armed Grunts > Armed Humanoid Replacements: Gun and Axe 1): each holds its own gun in its right hand and an axe in its left. They fire the gun at range, take to the axe when you walk up to them, and go back to the gun when you back off. Their guns hurt as much as the vanilla ones.
