# flat_visor_hit: see docs/vr-port/TESTING.md, "Checklist scenarios: how to write one". Flat-screen visor HUD: hits glitch the panels, low health pulses the health panel red (vr_flat_visor_damage, vr_flat_visor_low_health).
title Flat-screen visor HUD: hits and low health, two grunts shooting at you
hint Play with -flatscreenmode. Let the grunts shoot you: each hit glitches and shakes the panels for a moment, more for a bigger hit, the back plates lagging the faces, the numbers readable again at once. At 25 health and under the health panel pulses red, faster and stronger under 13. Kill them before you die; if you die the panels fade out and power on again at the respawn.
