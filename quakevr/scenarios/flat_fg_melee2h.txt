title Flat-screen force grab from a two-handed axe: an axe in the right hand, rocks to pull
hint Play with -flatscreenmode. Swing the axe (fire) until the left hand takes its handle, then look at a rock and press the grab key: the left hand slides off the handle first, then turns its palm out and pulls, as it does off a rocket launcher's fore-end. The same off the handle for the shove, G, 3 and a punch.
