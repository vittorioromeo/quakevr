title Thrown blades settle point first: a sword and a dagger to throw at grunts a few steps off
cvar vr_embed 1
map vrtesthall
god
notarget
give "Knight's Sword" main
give "Dagger" off
spawn "Grunt" 300 -60
spawn "Grunt" 300 60
hint Throw the sword and the dagger at the grunts, roughly point first: in the first few tenths of a second the blade steadies towards its point (not straight like an arrow) and sticks more often. A throw with a lot of tumble, or a badly aimed one, still tumbles; an axe flies as before. Debug > Tunables, Point-First Settling, sets how much (0: off).
