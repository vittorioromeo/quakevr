hint Pose Studio (Grip Editor > Drag Mode): walk up to one; pick a pose on the panel (< Pose >; a strike: < Key > and Play). Boxes on its weapon (blue), hands (orange), elbows (violet), chest (green); in Pickup its legs too: pelvis (tan), feet (pink), knees (blue-violet, their way): grip one, move and turn it, let go: it stays there in that pose. A hand on a weapon: how it holds it. Reset Pose, Copy to the other kind, Undo, Cancel, Done (prints the code). Live (bottom row): it fights in place (Loop, Speed); Pause, scrub with a stick or Next Glitch, then drag the key the readout names at that frame; Play goes on.
