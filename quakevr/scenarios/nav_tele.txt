# nav_tele: see docs/vr-port/TESTING.md, "Checklist scenarios: how to write one". e1m1's teleporter (the secret by the
# exit, one way to the start's side): a grunt beside it chases you, standing past its destination; it walks into the
# teleporter along a navmesh teleporter link (vr_nav_teleports) and comes out by you. Links drawn (vr_nav_debug 2:
# teleporter links cyan posts). The reach test's verdict line (nav_tele: 1 passed) as it comes within 160 of you after
# going through, 60 s at most.
title Navmesh: a grunt follows you through a teleporter
map e1m1
start 1312 1000 -380 90
god
spawn "Grunt" 0 96
cvar vr_nav 1
cvar vr_nav_debug 2
cvar vr_nav_teleports 1
cmd setpos -32 2000 -29
cmd vr_nav_reachtest nav_tele 160 60 tele monster_army
hint You stand past the teleporter's destination; the grunt by the teleporter at the far end of the level walks into it and comes out near you (teleporter links: cyan posts, its path yellow). With Navmesh: Teleporter Links off (Debug > Tunables, then reload) it takes the long way or none.
