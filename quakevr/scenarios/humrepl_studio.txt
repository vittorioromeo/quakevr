hint Five armed grunts before you, one per weapon. Open the menu: Debug > Tools > Texture Studio. Pick a category (Explosive: the rocket launcher's grunt) and move Red, Strength, Lightness and Tint: the grunt changes as you move them. Show the Masks: clothes green, pack blue, the rest (faces, arms, metal) dark red. Click a palette row to cycle it; Line Up another type. Paint (menu closed): point at a grunt, a yellow cursor shows the texels the brush would change (also on the skin's flat view, top right), the trigger paints, the grip erases, B/Y undoes; try hands, forearms and the back of a head. The lineup comes without backpacks or armour, with a backpack alone on a stand at its right end (Part: Pack to paint it); Hold Still and Turntable turn them round. Save writes humlook.txt and humlook_masks.txt.
