# agrunt_tanks: see docs/vr-port/TESTING.md, "Checklist scenarios: how to write one" (needs Arcane Dimensions)
title Armed grunts with a flamethrower and a poison sprayer, their tanks on their backs
map vrtesthall
god
notarget
give "Shotgun" main
spawn "Armed Grunt: Flamethrower" 150 90 0 0
spawn "Armed Grunt: Poison Sprayer" 150 -90 0 0
hint Their backs to you: tank on the back, hose to the gun. Shoot a tank (three hits): it bursts on the grunt (burning or poisoned, a field round it) and it drops its spent gun. Kill the other: its gun falls with its tank.
