# weapon attachments, the bayonet on the shotgun and the super shotgun.
title Bayonets: put them on the shotguns, stab, stick, fire and reload stuck
cvar vr_embed 1

map vrtesthall
god
notarget
give "Shotgun" main
spawn "Super Shotgun" 40 30
spawn "Bayonet" 40 -20
spawn "Bayonet" 40 -35
spawn "Ogre" 140 -60
spawn "Ogre" 140 60
hint Take a bayonet in your left hand and bring its socket to the shotgun's muzzle, under the barrel: it snaps on. Stab an ogre: it sticks; keep your hand on the gun and pull the trigger: it fires there. Grip the bayonet with the empty hand and pull it off. The same with the super shotgun (break it open, load it). Holster, drop and throw a gun with its bayonet: it stays on. Flat-screen: H takes a bayonet in the free hand, J puts it on (or takes it off), V stabs with it, fire shoots.
