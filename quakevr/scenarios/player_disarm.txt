# player_disarm: see docs/vr-port/TESTING.md, "Checklist scenarios: how to write one". Enemies' shots on your gun can knock it out of your hand (his note 2026-11-15 03:28; vr_pd_test 1).
title You can be disarmed: grunts and an enforcer shooting at you, your gun held out
cvar vr_player_disarm 1
cvar vr_player_disarm_chance 1
map vrtesthall
god
give "Shotgun" main
spawn "Grunt" 400 -80
spawn "Grunt" 400 80
spawn "Enforcer" 500 0
hint Hold your shotgun out in front of you towards the grunts and let them shoot: shots that hit the gun or the hand holding it add up, and now and then the gun is knocked out of your hand (sparks, a ricochet, a buzz, a flash). It drops where you can pick it up again with its clip. Shots on your body never disarm, nor do fists or an empty hand. Advanced VR Options > Combat > Armed Grunts, Disarming: Your Weapon, turns it off or scales the chance.
