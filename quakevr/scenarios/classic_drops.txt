# classic_drops: see docs/vr-port/TESTING.md, "Checklist scenarios: how to write one". Classic's gameplay on e1m1 (the
# rows that matter here: recoil on, no enemy weapon drops, plain grunts with id's shotgun) and three plain grunts ahead;
# a shotgun in your hand for the recoil and a grunt's burst rifle in the other (it bursts whatever the grunts fire).
title Classic: guns recoil, grunts drop backpacks and no weapons
map e1m1
god
give "Shotgun" main
give "Grunt Burst Rifle" off
cvar vr_weapon_recoil 1
cvar vr_enemy_held_drops 0
cvar vr_enemy_drops 2
cvar vr_agrunt_repl 0
cvar vr_grunt_burst 0
spawn "Grunt" 260 -60
spawn "Grunt" 300 0
spawn "Grunt" 260 60
hint Fire the shotgun: it kicks in your hand. Kill the grunts: each drops a backpack, and its gun falls only as scenery you can't pick up, fading after a few seconds. The burst rifle in your other hand fires 3-round bursts.
