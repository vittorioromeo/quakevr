# flat_swim: see docs/vr-port/TESTING.md, "Checklist scenarios: how to write one". Flat-screen (-flatscreenmode) swimming strokes (Quake/vr/vr_flatswim.cpp; docs/vr-port/FLATPLAY.md, "Swimming").
title Flat-screen swimming: the vrtesthall pool, a nailgun held two-handed
map vrtesthall
start 150 -264 40 0
god
notarget
give "Nailgun" main
hint Play with -flatscreenmode. Walk into the pool and swim: forward (the breaststroke), back, sideways, up (jump) and down; stay still to tread water. The nailgun's free hand lets go and swims; fire and reload under water: the gun hand's stroke gives way and comes back. Climb out: the free hand goes back on the nailgun. Debug > Tunables > Flatscreen > Swimming tunes it.
