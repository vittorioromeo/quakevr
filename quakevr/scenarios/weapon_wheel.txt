# weapon_wheel: see docs/vr-port/TESTING.md, "Checklist scenarios: how to write one". The headset's weapon wheel (his 2026-11-23: vr_weaponwheel; Quake/vr/vr_weaponwheel.cpp, QC vr_flatholster.qc VR_Wheel_Pick).
title VR weapon wheel: hold B or Y, move the hand to a holster's slot, let go
hint Put the shotgun and the nailgun in holsters, take the weapons lying ahead too. Hold B (or Y) about a fifth of a second: a wheel of your holsters opens round that hand and time slows (no menu sound). Walk with the stick while it is open: it comes along. Move the hand to a slot (a tick in the controller) and let go: that hand takes it, what it held goes back to its holster. EMPTY HAND puts it away; letting go in the middle or off the wheel does nothing. (The wheel is turned on for this scene: VR Settings, Weapon Wheel.)
