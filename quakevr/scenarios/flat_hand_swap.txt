# flat_hand_swap: see docs/vr-port/TESTING.md, "Checklist scenarios: how to write one". Flat-screen (-flatscreenmode) hand swap animation (3: vr_flatswap.cpp; docs/vr-port/FLATPLAY.md, "The hand swap").
title Flat-screen hand swap: a shotgun, then a rocket launcher held in both hands
hint Play with -flatscreenmode. 3 with the shotgun in your right hand: both hands come together, the shotgun changes hands where they meet, and they go back. Take the rocket launcher (two hands): 3 first lets the left hand go of its grip to the empty hand's pose in front of you (the gun stays put), then the right hand brings the gun across to it and the swap happens there, then the right hand goes onto the gun's grip. With a gun in each hand they meet and swap too.
