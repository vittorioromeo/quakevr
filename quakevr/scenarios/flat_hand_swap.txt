title Flat-screen hand swap: a shotgun, then a nail gun held in both hands
hint Play with -flatscreenmode. 3 with the shotgun in your right hand: both hands come together, the shotgun changes hands where they meet, and they go back. Take the nail gun (two hands): 3 takes the left hand off its grip to the right hand, the swap happens there, and the right hand goes onto the grip. With a gun in each hand they meet and swap too.
