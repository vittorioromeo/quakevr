// quakevr/data/reloads.txt -- flat-screen play's procedural reloads, per gun (vr_reloadsys.hpp; written by the Reload
// Studio, vr_reloadstudio). Keys play in order; `loop a b` repeats keys a..b until the gun is full; `finish 1` keys
// are the reload's end. hand: rest | pouch | mouth | dest | hold; action: none | take | insert | open | close;
// if: always | closed | opened | loaded; ease: linear | smooth | in | out. Offsets in units (forward, right, up),
// turns in degrees (pitch, yaw, roll); the free hand's mirrored by its side, the gun's by its hand's.

reload shotgun

    dest port
    item_rot -38.17 -43.74 51.87

    key "reach"

        time 0.2
        gun_time 0.17
