# Quake VR playtest checklist (VR menu > Checklist). One line = one thing to try: what to do and what to look for; tick what works, tell me what doesn't.
# Reload List refreshes this page; no rebuild needed. Ticked items are retired (their marks stay in checklist_ticks.txt); older items are in git history.
# Sections are by feature (Dwell, Quoth, Arcane Dimensions, Multiplayer and bots, Flat screen, Ropes and chains, Weapons and holsters, Armed humanoids, ragdolls and physics, Tutorial and VR interface, Survival Arena, Installer, start-up and platforms, Recording and dev tools); empty ones are left out and come back when a line needs them.
# A flat-only line starts "Flat-screen:"; a line that works in either mode starts "Flat-screen or VR:" (try it in either); the rest are for the headset.
# New lines from a ship land in a [New: not yet sorted] section at the top (or in the section a lines file names on its first line, "[section: <name>]"); the next sort moves them into place and drops that section.
# A line ending in {test:<id>} has a Test button: it loads quakevr/scenarios/<id>.txt's scene (docs/vr-port/TESTING.md, "Checklist scenarios"); every line ends in one (at least a minimal scene: the map, where you stand, what is given). Multiplayer lines' scenes start a local game with bots (passive: stand still, never attack, take hits; or active), nothing opened on the network. Flat-screen lines' scenes work under -flatscreenmode too (the game's mode is picked at start-up, not by the scene).
# Lines are only for something new or changed you can see or feel: one line per flow, never "works as before". History of the sorts: git log of this file and docs/vr-port/CHECKLIST_REORG.md (latest: 2026-11-22: 12 ticked or superseded lines retired, the [New] section sorted into Ropes and chains, Arcane Dimensions and Tutorial and VR interface, empty sections dropped; before that 2026-11-14 release audit, 94 lines down to 68, 71 with three from a later ship: the [New] section sorted, near-duplicates merged, every line cut to what to do and what to see, 3 new scenes; 2026-11-15: 17 ticked lines retired, the [New] section sorted). Misc/quakevr/checklist_tests_check.py checks every line has a tag, every tag's scene file and the format (no "(agent/", "Test:", scratch or qvr-agents paths, .sh names; sections in order).
