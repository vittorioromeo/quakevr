# Quake VR playtest checklist (VR menu > Checklist). One line = one thing to try: what to do and what to look for; tick what works, tell me what doesn't.
# Reload List refreshes this page; no rebuild needed. Ticked items are retired (their marks stay in checklist_ticks.txt); older items are in git history.
# Sections run in play-test order: the flat-screen sections first (what you'd try at the desk: flat weapons, the run setup, menus and the Map Library), then the headset's (body, melee, weapons, physics, the hub, the arena, recording and dev tools). A line that works in either mode sits where it is most natural to test. A line's text is the key of its tick: reword a line and it is untested again.
# A flat-only line starts "Flat-screen:"; a line that works in either mode starts "Flat-screen or VR:" (try it in either); the rest are for the headset.
# New lines from a ship land in a [New: not yet sorted] section at the top (or in the section a lines file names on its first line, "[section: <name>]"); the next sort moves them into place and drops that section.
# A line ending in {test:<id>} has a Test button: it loads quakevr/scenarios/<id>.txt's scene (docs/vr-port/TESTING.md, "Checklist scenarios"); give new lines one whenever a scene fits. Flat-screen lines' scenes work under -flatscreenmode too (the game's mode is picked at start-up, not by the scene).
# Lines are only for something new or changed you can see or feel: one line per flow, never "works as before". History of the sorts: git log of this file and docs/vr-port/CHECKLIST_REORG.md (latest: 2026-11-14 release audit, 94 lines down to 68, 71 with three from a later ship: the [New] section sorted, near-duplicates merged, every line cut to what to do and what to see, 3 new scenes). Misc/quakevr/checklist_tests_check.py checks every tag's scene file and the format (no "(agent/", "Test:", scratch or qvr-agents paths, .sh names; sections in order).

[1. Flat-screen: weapons and hands]
Flat-screen: hold the axe (then a sword, the crowbar, Mjolnir, the super axe) with the left hand empty: the left hand goes onto the handle by itself and the weapon sits two-handed; take something in the left hand: back to one hand, no jumps either way (Debug > Tunables > Flatscreen > Two-Handed Melee Stance). {test:flat_melee2h}
Flat-screen: in the holster menu (middle mouse), drag a weapon with the left button between hands and holsters: valid slots light up, the rest grey; hand to hand or holster to holster swaps; letting go on nothing cancels. {test:flat_holster_drag}
Flat-screen or VR: shotgun in the right hand, a spent flare gun in the left: swap hands, drop, holster, draw, reload and swap mid-reload in any order, pulling both triggers after each: an empty shotgun only clicks, a loaded one fires one shell, never "ERROR: weapon clip less than zero". {test:flat_clip_unpaid}

Menu Look > Run Setup: Icon Pickers off gives plain rows; Icon Picker Size and Unpicked Dim change them at once. {test:run_setup_newgame}
Flat-screen or VR: the icon pickers (Run Setup's Difficulty, Starting Gun, Enemy Count) are shorter, the pictures keep their proportions (not stretched) and a name on two lines is tightly spaced; Menu Look > Icon Picker Size still resizes them. {test:run_setup_newgame}
Flat-screen or VR: Run Setup shows the Start button right under the Difficulty picker, with Presets, Rules and Mutators below it. {test:run_setup_newgame}
Flat-screen or VR: Levels > e1m1's Run Setup opens at its top with Difficulty in view (also after Select Campaign's Run Setup); Bloody Nightmare is a fifth picture after Nightmare: Start it and you get Nightmare with Bloody Nightmare's rules, and the save's Continue line says Bloody Nightmare.
Flat-screen or VR: change Enemy Count, Randomized Enemies and Randomized Pickups, then start from Levels, the Map Library, New Game, a campaign or the hub: the map has exactly those, never an earlier run's; the Firing Range button uses them too.
Flat-screen or VR: Randomized Pickups, Balanced: ammo, armour and weapons become close ones, health stays health, keys and door items stay put. {test:run_pickups}
Flat-screen or VR: Glass Cannon: punches and swings at the firing range's dummy show twice the damage, not only shots; Melee Only: its guns and ammo can't be taken. {test:run_glass_dummy}
Flat-screen or VR: Classic Quake (or Style Classic): no gun ever needs reloading, and e1m1's grunts are all plain shotgun grunts.
Flat-screen or VR: Melee Only with the dagger, "maxplayers 2" and a bot (impulse 100): the bot holds the dagger, skips guns and ammo, and fights with the dagger.

[3. Flat-screen: menus, campaigns and the Map Library]
Flat-screen or VR: play a campaign until it autosaves, quit to the menu and open Select Campaign: a "Continue (N min ago)" row under that campaign, its run on the line below, loads straight into the save with no Run Setup; campaigns without an autosave have none.
Flat-screen or VR: Map Library, an installed package you have autosaved in: a "Continue (N min ago): <run>" button above Uninstall / Reinstall loads its newest autosave; a package never played has none, and Play still opens Run Setup.
Flat-screen or VR: Select Campaign reads at a glance: big white titles, a dim tag only when a campaign isn't ready, Continue indented under its campaign with a dim age, Arcane Dimensions under an Experimental header, a small footer; hover and selection clear on titles and Continue rows.
Flat-screen or VR: Search a setting on a long page (Arm Drip Rate) and open it: the page lands with that row in the middle, glowing for about 1.5 s (Menu Look > Search Result Flash, Search Flash Strength).
Flat-screen: on a long page (Locomotion) one mouse wheel notch scrolls a quarter of the rows and the selection stays on the page (Menu Look > Mouse Wheel Scroll; 0: row by row).
Flat-screen or VR: the main menu's selected row has a soft bar as faint as the settings pages' highlight (Menu Look > Big Menu Bar Opacity).
Flat-screen or VR: walk forward and open the menu with Live Preview on: you stop at once, and after closing it you don't walk until you press forward again.

Installer: install into a fresh folder and pick Snap 45, Left-handed and Medium on "Set up the game": the game has them. Change Turning in the game, run Update, change only Turn Speed: Turning stays yours.

[4. VR: body and gear]
With armour on, set Reloading > X With Armour and Flashlight > Forward With Armour to about 3 units (the scene does): the pouch and the belt torch stand clear of the plates, and go back when the armour is gone. {test:armour_gear}
VR: teleport many times quickly (headset at 90 or 120 Hz): every release teleports you.

[5. VR: melee and embedded blades]
Stab a standing monster with the sword without staggering it, keep holding, and let it walk into a teleporter: the sword comes back to your hand, not held from across the map. {test:embed_tele}
Stab a grunt deep and hold the sword: swing sideways, pull back, lift it overhead: the body (alive or dead) follows your hand a little behind and hangs on the blade; an axe a bit less, an ogre much less. Blade Hold Strength x0.25 lags and sags, x10 pulls the body up the blade and round with your wrist. {test:embed_stab}
Hold a sword left in a dead or staggering grunt by its handle and lift a little, turning your wrist: the sword and the drawn hand stay on your real hand, and the body comes with the blade; grip it a little off the handle: the handle comes to your hand in a fifth of a second. {test:embed_handle}
Grip the handle of a sword left in a crate or a body, the blade rolled or tilted any way: the drawn hand lies on the handle exactly as it holds that sword normally. {test:embed_handle}
Stab a standing grunt hard enough to stagger it and keep hold: if your hand went past the hilt or stopped short, the body slides along the blade until the handle is in your hand (Debug > Tunables > Moving: Held by the Handle, Moving: Handle Comes to the Hand). {test:embed_handle}
Stab a wooden crate and keep hold: move your hand aside and the crate comes along with the handle in your hand; lifting with one hand only tips it, and turning your wrist doesn't swing it about. {test:embed_crates}
Leave a blade in a crate or a barrel and push or pull the crate with your other hand or your body: the blade goes with it, where it went in. {test:embed_crates}
With a gib, a head or a limb skewered on your sword, reach for it with your free hand: it takes it only at the gib itself, and the hand is drawn on the gib's surface, not floating off it. {test:skewer_grip}
Run at a grunt and throw the knight's sword or the dagger hard, point first: it dies whole, falling with about 80% of the blade in its body; with Quad it still gibs, and handle-first or flat throws are as before. {test:throw_nogib}
Hold the dagger or a sword and press B (Y on the off hand): it turns half round in a fifth of a second with a click, blade down, thumb over the pommel; press again to flip it back. {test:revgrip}
Toss the dagger so it turns over and catch it blade down: you hold it reversed (caught blade up: upright); a force grab takes it upright; holstered and drawn, a reversed blade stays reversed. {test:revgrip}
Held blade down, stab the grunt from above: the blade sticks in, and your hand on its handle looks the same as holding it. {test:revgrip}

[6. VR: weapons and grips]
Pick up the dagger (in your main hand here, the knight's sword in the other to compare): the sword's clean steel, a third of its blade, a smaller guard and grip, sitting in your hand as the sword does. {test:dagger}
Swing and stab the grunt with the dagger: a stab sticks a third as deep as the sword's and holds the body as a sword stab does, a throw sticks point first, and it hurts about 0.6 of the sword (Combat > Weapon Damage > Melee > Dagger). {test:dagger}
Stab a severed head and a limb onto the dagger: each sits a little back from the tip, short of the hilt, and a second gib won't go on while one is on it (the sword still takes three). {test:dagger}
Let the armed grunt with the dagger fight you, then disarm it: it holds and swings the dagger one-handed as grunts hold swords, and picks it up again. {test:dagger}
Flat-screen or VR: daggers lie beside the two swords in the firing range and in the toolgun's Weapons list; holster one and throw one as you would the sword. {test:dagger}
The super axe's blade shape and muzzle and the slot 36 torch offsets are your tuned values: they look as you left them. {test:superaxe_blade}
Clip the flashlight on the super shotgun and break it open: the torch and its beam drop with the barrels instead of the barrels swinging through it. {test:ssg_open_torch}
Flat-screen or VR: load shells into the open super shotgun: their brass heads sit flush with the breech face, not sunk in the bores; an empty chamber shows its open bore. {test:ssg_open_torch}
Flat-screen or VR: fire the grenade launcher and force-grab the lit grenade out of the air: its fuse restarts at the full 2.5 s with a re-arm click and sparks, one fizz and tick stream from the top, and you can throw it again. {test:grenade_countdown}
Flat-screen or VR: look down the rocket launcher from your hand: the three vents at the back of the front shroud are real holes, black inside with walls and a back; the floor pickup shows them too. {test:rl_vents}
VR: a very quick trigger or grip press and release still fires or grabs.
VR: let go of a gun and grab it again quickly: the re-grab always takes it.

Push a grunt and a hell knight gently off a high ledge so they grab its lip: the grunt climbs onto the top without its hips sinking back onto the edge, and the hell knight gets its second hand onto the lip as it pulls up. {test:rag_hang}

[9. VR: Survival Arena (Play > Survival Arena)]
Play the arena a while: it feels good and each new wave starts without a hitch; note anything to improve. {test:arena_run}
Survival Arena from wave 6 (Normal): grunts and enforcers bring the heavy revolver, burst rifle, laser rifle, the knights' swords, chainsaw, Mjolnir and the Super Axe, each first on one foe at its wave; their drops are yours to fire. {test:arena_newguns}
Survival Arena with Arcane Dimensions' data: from wave 11 (Normal) the waves also bring AD's soldiers' guns, the Widowmaker, Shadow Axe and Plasma Gun, and AD's swords, hammer and mace.
Survival Arena budget director (on by default: Combat > Survival Arena > Budget Director; docs/vr-port/ARENA_DIRECTOR.md):
- Start a run without changing anything: each countdown names the wave's theme ("Wave 3 in 3... Melee Rush"); with Budget Director off the next run plays the classic waves. {test:arena_run}
- Waves change every run and follow their theme: a Melee Rush brings blades and dogs, a Ranged Siege guns from far, an Elite a few armoured big ones. {test:arena_gunknights}
- Spawns come in small groups with a pause after each: kill an arrival quickly and the next waits, dawdle and they pile up; once or twice a wave a horn and "SURGE!" (or "AMBUSH!" behind you) brings a bigger group. {test:arena_run}
- From about wave 12 / 18 knights and death knights carry real guns, fire them and drop them; a Knightfall wave is mostly them; a boss wave may bring a death knight in red armour with the best gun so far. {test:arena_gunknights}
- A kill within 4 s of the foe arriving is a quick kill: the wave clear line says "N quick kills: +score". {test:arena_run}
- A clear's supplies grow with the wave and lean on how you're doing: badly hurt, more medkits (a megahealth when very low); untouched, now and then a quad, a ring or late a pentagram; red armour from wave 22.
Debug > Tools > Arena Wave Editor: build a wave with an armed grunt, dual axes, red armour and infected with shambler, save it, assign it to Normal and play from wave 1: the panel readable, laser, stick scrolling and panel keys work. {test:vrarena_calm}

[10. VR: recording and dev tools]
Takes: with OBS running, press TAKE on the wrist gadget, act, press STOP: a small blinking red light in the headset only, then the rating prompt; KEEP renames OBS's file to <date>_<scene>_t01_keep, REJECT moves it to rejected/. Left grip + X then Y marks in and out (vr_take_list shows them, X / Y do nothing in game); SET UP, knock props about, RETAKE: the scene comes back and a new take starts. {test:takes}
Takes, Clip That: in a take session press CLIP (or F8): OBS's folder gets a <date>_<scene>_tNN_clip file of the last seconds, rated like a take. Flat-screen: F7 starts and stops a take (a REC line top right), keypad 1 / 2 / 3 rate it, F8 clips, Home / PgUp mark in and out. {test:takes}
Takes: record a voice note in a take, shoot a few takes with marks, rate some keep and maybe, transcribe, press END: OBS's folder gets session_<date>/ with the files, session.json and timeline.fcpxml, which Resolve imports as the keep takes trimmed to their marks, then the maybe ones, the notes as markers. {test:takes}
Start the Armour Studio near an armed grunt, then leave the menu (or close the studio's window): the grunt fights again and your blows hurt it. {test:armourstudio_close}
Debug > Embedded Blades: Why, in slow motion: each stab says why it stuck or not, why it came out and how far a miss was off, with a sound when a blade stays in or comes out. {test:embed_stab}
Show Melee Arcs: each blow's own rule is drawn (a spawn's leap ring, the dragon's tail all round, a vore's shove cone, armed grunts' arcs). {test:agrunt_melee_spacing}
