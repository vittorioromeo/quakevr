# Quake VR playtest checklist: what to test in the headset or give feedback on.
# Shown in the VR menu by the "Checklist" corner button (also Debug > Checklist); read at runtime, so edit this
# file and the game picks it up (within a second, or "Reload List" on the page). No rebuild.
#
# Format:
#   [Section name]      a header; the items under it are listed in that section
#   one item per line   the whole line is the item (long ones wrap in the menu)
#   # or //             at the start of a line: a comment; blank lines are ignored
#
# Ticks are kept in checklist_ticks.txt next to this file (not committed), by each item's exact text: add, move or
# remove items freely; changing an item's wording un-ticks it.

[Test in VR]
Grenade pouch: the grenade's angle at the new defaults (pitch -180, yaw 90)
Prop area (vrfiringrange, south-west corner): table height, tiny rocks easy to grab?
Prop area: is the torch wall too bright?
Checklist page: laser and sticks on multi-line items, dimming, icon
Batting by weight: a weak flick barely moves a heavy box; light things still bat well (Carrying: Hand/Weapon Mass, Push Force)
Held props in both hands meet, with up to 5 cm of give and a buzz (Held Things Collide, Collide Give)
Held prop swung through monsters never drops
Flung props: turn Hurt Players on, bat props close to your body and flail; nothing should hit you as it leaves
Flung prop damage feels right (Throwing and Physics > Flung Props: Damage, Reference Mass)
Punches land instantly on the dummy and on monsters
Close sword or axe cuts: does "pommel" show up wrongly? (Melee > Pommel Strike Wait 0.05, 0.1 = old)
Empty hammer is silent when you squeeze the trigger
Hitbox prototype: Debug > Tests > Hitbox Width 20, then walls, corners, stairs, slopes, doors, lifts, trains and climbing
Tired arms at 15 kg feel right

Grapple: hook stays in after releasing the trigger; back button detaches only; reel in with Y/B
Grapple: front button (near the muzzle) reels in instantly; is it well placed? (sliders: vr_grapple_front_button_x/y/z)
Grapple: shoot a stuck hook to free it
Grapple: rope never goes through walls or floors, doesn't tangle, always reels in from the floor
Grapple: hang the gun from the ceiling; it settles within a couple of seconds
Grapple: walking with a loose hook or hooked prop; does it trail naturally? (Move Share 0.7)
Grapple: flail spin is no longer overpowered (Load Drag, Load Max Speed)
Grapple: taut rope with a stuck end stops you moving away (max length 1500)
Grapple: loose hook points the right way; rope starts at the muzzle, in hand and dropped
Grapple: hook only bites what you actually hit (armour, props)
Grapple: slack rope looks right (Debug > Views > Show Grapple Rope shows the points)

Held prop: one hand, then both, pull one off and back (each hand); it stays on the kept hand, regrab where drawn
Held prop: in both hands, open one; it slides onto the kept hand with no gap
Force grab props while walking: no gap in the hand

Melee: soft punches do nothing; real jabs, hooks and uppercuts land
Melee: reloading or waving a gun near the dummy doesn't hit; pistol-whip and butt strikes do
Melee: wiggling a sword or axe against the dummy doesn't hit; real cuts land
