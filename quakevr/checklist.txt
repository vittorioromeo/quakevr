Skewer a gib on the sword and throw it hard flat at a wall (side on, so the blade's flat meets it): the gib bursts on the wall; thrown gently it stays on.
Flat-screen: record a take (vr_take) with the default settings: no REC corner line or rating prompt shows in the window. Turn on Recording > "Recording Light and Prompt (Flat Screen)" and the corner line appears.

Flat-screen: a grenade grabbed with E (a dud you dropped, or an ogre's live one) comes into the hand held out as after G: a dud needs fire to arm it, then fire held winds up and letting go throws; a live one throws straight away. {test:flat_grenade_grab}
Flat-screen: cut a head or limb onto the axe, then turn the view fast with the mouse several times: it stays on the blade; a swing of the axe (mouse button) that stops flings it off.
Flat-screen or VR: in a big fight with lots of blood on screen, shoot a corpse up close: its blood spray always appears (New Particles Replace Oldest on in Graphics, Effects).
Flat-screen: hold the super shotgun, the nailgun or the axe alone and press 3: the left hand lets go to the empty hand's pose, the weapon is passed to it, then the right hand comes back beside it, the same steps as the rocket launcher. {test:flat_swap_props}
Flat-screen: with a prop in one hand and a gun in the other (or two props, or a prop and an empty hand) press 3: the hands meet and exchange what they hold smoothly, the prop never vanishing on the way. {test:flat_swap_props}
Flat-screen or VR: with an AD soldier's gun in your right hand (the Plasma Soldier's Rifle, even without AD installed), press 3 (in VR: pass it to the other hand, or holster and draw it): it changes hands and stays the rifle, never gone. {test:flat_swap_adgun}
Flat-screen: hold the super shotgun, the shotgun, the nailgun or an AD gun alone and press 3: as with the rocket launcher, the left hand visibly lets go of the gun, the gun is passed to it, then the right hand reaches onto the gun's front during the swap, not late after it. {test:flat_swap_props}
Flat-screen: hold the axe (then a sword, the crowbar, Mjolnir, the super axe) with the left hand empty: it goes onto the handle by itself without swinging and the weapon sits in a two-handed stance; take a weapon or a prop in the left hand and it eases back to the one-handed hold, with no jumps either way. Tune it in Debug > Tunables > Flatscreen > Two-Handed Melee Stance. {test:flat_melee2h}
Flat-screen: from the two-handed stance swing, parry with the right mouse button (the Two-Handed Guard rows move only this guard), shove and force grab: the left hand lets go of the handle smoothly and takes it again after. {test:flat_melee2h}
Flat-screen or VR: with TAA on (Flat TAA, or the headset's TAA), shoot a limb off a body right next to you and keep the view still: the stump's blood stream shows at once, not only after you move or turn. {test:flat_taa_limbpop}
Flat-screen: hold the axe alone and press 3 to swap hands, then back, with the two-handed stance on and off: the axe and both hands stay in view every moment of the swap (no vanishing under the view right after it), and the same with the sword, crowbar and chainsaw. {test:flat_swap_melee}

[3. VR: body, lying down and crouching]
Lie down and crawl with your arms: a hand drags you only when its knuckles are on the floor (Arm Crawl Floor Height 1, steps of 0.25, 0 allowed); with Empty Hand Crawls When = Closed (default) an open empty hand on the floor does nothing and a fist (grip or trigger) drags you, Open and Either do as named, the shotgun hand always drags; pull back to go forward, push ahead to go back, pull sideways to slide sideways. {test:prone_floor}
Lying Down Below goes up to 48: try 26-30 (on all fours) and see you lie down without lying flat; getting up still works, and Getting Up Margin (default 2) stops flicker if your head bobs at the limit. {test:prone_floor}
Crawl into the 40-high tunnel lying down and get up: only to a crouch (with Lowest Crouched Height 44 you stay lying until you crawl out). Crouch for real into it and stand up inside: you stay crouched and can't move, the view goes nearly black and blurred and "Crouch back down" shows; crouch again and it clears and you walk on. Is the darkness right, and does it let go at a comfortable height? {test:prone_tunnel}
Lie down, crawl into the 20-high vent ahead and get up for real inside it: you stay lying and can't move (stick, arm crawl, a step in the room, teleport), the view goes nearly black and blurred and "Lie back down" shows in front of you, the teleporter in the wall above never takes you even with your hands up; lie back down and it all clears and you crawl on. {test:lowceil_vent}
Get hurt with your hands near your face: your blood streams, splashes and drips still show right at your face (Your Gore: Eye Clearance now 0), and each stream starts on your drawn body or arm, moving with it, not floating off it. {test:playergore}

[4. VR: melee, embedded blades and holding]
Skewer a gib or a head on the sword, then snap turn, turn smoothly with the stick and walk: it stays locked on the blade the whole time, with no jump, lag or flicker. {test:vr_skewer_snap}
Pull a stuck blade along its axis: it slides (harder the deeper) and comes out 1:1, hand and blade gliding back together; an axe only past its depth plus 20 cm with a firm pull (slow: your hand comes off), shaking and buzzing as you near it; hard sideways frees it, across it moves the body and only a fast yank tears it out. {test:embed_stab}
Open your hand: the blade stays in, bleeding; grip it: back on the same axis, no ghost hand; force grab from afar tugs it out to you; the second hand takes the two-handed grip and stays; turning your hand turns a stuck axe a few degrees, a sword less; a sunk handle turns out to be grabbed. {test:embed_stab}
Kill a grunt with the blade held: you keep it, a slow pull carries the corpse (overhead on a sword, dragged by an axe), a firm tug frees it, gibbed it's back in your hand; on a falling body your arm stretches, then lets go. Behead or de-limb with the axe, or cut off a part holding a stuck axe: the axe never floats where it was. {test:embed_stab}
Stab hard: the grunt staggers about 2.5 s and pulling drags it, deep and two-handed it's off balance about 3.5 s; a weak stab: no stagger. The axe swung very hard: about 0.75 s. Ogre: shorter; shambler: a moment, no drag, you slowed; walk away holding a blade: you drag it at a third of your speed, out past arm's reach. {test:embed_stab}
A wound with a blade in it bleeds: sliding the blade makes flesh sounds and spurts every 3 cm, live or dead; held past 10 s, a stream follows the body, stopping when the blade leaves or the body dies. {test:embed_stab}
Kill a grunt messily, stab a gib or its head tip first: it rides the blade (up to 3), the blade heavier; the other hand slides one off or takes it; a slash bursts a loose one; dropped or switched they stay or fall unbroken; a hard gib-first throw or the gib's part hitting a wall bursts it. {test:embed_stab}
An axe swing that cuts off a head or limb keeps it on the axe at the cut (it adds a little weight); a hard stopped swing or a sharp shake flings it off; the other hand pulls it off (about 30 cm); swords don't keep parts (vr_embed_cuton 2). {test:embed_stab}
Throw a sword into a grunt: it bleeds; kill it and the blade rides the corpse; gib it or cut off that limb: the blade falls. Combat > Embedded Blades off: only axes stick, falling out when the body dies. {test:embed_throw}
Stab a grunt deep and hold the sword: swing your hand sideways, pull it back, lift it over your head with one hand pointing the sword up: the staggering body (and a dead one) follows your hand closely, a little behind, and hangs on the blade overhead; an axe the same, a bit less; an ogre much harder. Blade Hold Strength (Advanced VR Options > Combat > Embedded Blades, and VR Settings > Gameplay): at x0.25 it lags and sags, at x10 the body comes all the way up the blade and swings round with your wrist. {test:embed_stab}
Stab a crate hard with the sword, or swing the axe hard into a barrel: the blade stays in. Holding it, move aside: the crate drags along, heavily; lift: one hand only tips it, both lift it. Open your hand: it stays in and rides the crate pushed away; grip it again and pull it straight out (harder than out of a body). Break the crate: the blade falls free, back in your hand if held. {test:embed_crates}
Run into a grunt with a held stab: the blade pushes back and you stop in front of it instead of running past (Tunables: Running Stab: Brake, Running Stab: Speed). {test:embed_stab}
Stab a grunt and keep holding while it goes active ragdoll: the grip holds (Blade Hold Strength x1; raise it if it still slips). {test:embed_stab}
Cut a limb or head off onto the blade, then punch it or strike it with the other hand's weapon: it bursts with a spray of blood right where it sat on the blade, not just small gibs; if the blood still doesn't show with the blade close to your face, lower Particle Fade Near Eyes and try again. {test:embed_stab}
Swing and chop an axe or sword at a grunt's chest and shoulders: it dies without losing its head; a cut across the neck still beheads it, and the training dummy says "would behead" only for blows it calls a head hit.
Swing the axe hard into a dead grunt's ragdoll: the axe stays in it (grab it back out) instead of the body bursting into gibs.
Chop and slash with the Super Axe's blade: the blows land as the head (and stay in when hard enough), not as pommel strikes; the white blow line with Show Melee Blades ends at the middle of the blade's edge. {test:superaxe_blade}
Stab the training dummy with the sword and chop the axe into a grunt so they stay in, then pull them out: the blade is bloody where it went in, and stays bloody in your hand.
In VR, force grab a brick or a gib: the beam starts from your palm, and the thing stops tumbling as it arrives and sits in the hand upright as it lay.
Neck snap standing and on a lifted body: it goes limp, still held. Leg grabs: pull one down by the shins, drag it by the legs, lift it by the legs. Hold one with both hands: no crash. {test:grunts_to_throw}

[7. Survival Arena (Play > Survival Arena)]
The arena is nice: play it, feels good? Note improvements. {test:arena_run}
Survival Arena from wave 6 on (Normal): grunts and enforcers now come with the heavy revolver, the burst rifle, the enforcer's laser rifle, the knight's and death knight's swords, the chainsaw, Mjolnir and the Super Axe, each first on one foe at its own wave and then in the mix; their drops are yours to pick up and fire. {test:arena_newguns}
Survival Arena with Arcane Dimensions' data: from wave 11 (Normal) the waves also bring AD's soldiers' guns (defender's shotgun, crossbow, grenade and rocket launchers, plasma rifle, flamethrower, centurion's plasma gun, poison sprayer), the Widowmaker, Shadow Axe and Plasma Gun, and AD's swords, hammer and mace.
Survival Arena budget director (on by default: Combat > Survival Arena > Budget Director; docs/vr-port/ARENA_DIRECTOR.md):
- Start a run without changing any setting: the budget director runs it (each countdown names the wave's theme, such as "Wave 3 in 3... Melee Rush"); turn Budget Director off and the next run plays exactly the classic waves. {test:arena_run}
- A run's waves change every run, and a wave's foes follow its theme (a Melee Rush brings blades and dogs, a Ranged Siege guns from far, an Elite few armoured big ones). {test:arena_gunknights}
- Spawns come in small groups with a pause after each; kill an arrival quickly and the next group waits for you, dawdle and they pile up. Once or twice a wave a "SURGE!" (or "AMBUSH!", behind you) with a horn brings a bigger group from several sides at once.
- Knights and death knights carry real guns from about wave 12 / 18 (shotguns, nailguns, launchers), fire them at you and drop them when they fall; a Knightfall wave is mostly them; a boss wave can bring a death knight in red armour with the best gun so far (a rocket launcher, a laser cannon). {test:arena_gunknights}
- A kill within 4 s of the foe coming in counts as a quick kill: the wave clear line says "N quick kills: +score".
- A clear's supplies grow with the wave beaten and lean on how you're doing: badly hurt, more medkits (a megahealth when very low); untouched, now and then a quad, a ring or (late) a pentagram; red armour from wave 22.
Debug > Tools > Arena Wave Editor: build a wave with an armed grunt, dual axes, red armour and infected with shambler; save, assign it to Normal, Play from wave 1: the panel readable, laser, stick scrolling and panel keys work. {test:vrarena_calm}

During a take hold the left grip and press X then Y: vr_take_list shows an in and an out mark there, and X / Y did nothing in the game.
