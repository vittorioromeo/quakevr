# Quake VR playtest checklist (VR menu > Checklist). One line = one quick check; tick what works, tell me what doesn't.
# Merged and streamlined 2026-10-06 from the previous list and docs/vr-port/CHECKLIST_ARCHIVE_20261005.txt
# (both kept in git history). Reload List refreshes this page; no rebuild needed.

[New this week]
Head pops: shotgun and super shotgun headshot kills pop up close, rarer with range (shotgun sooner), never far away; lightning always; heavy thrown things pop (Gore > Decapitation > Head Pop Chance)
Lightning: one hit puts arcs on a living enemy that last through its death; corpses convulse until the arcs end; try Duration 30 s; arcs readable, lights not too strong (Gore > Lightning Shock)
Shove toward a high ledge: always knocks down; Ledge Reach and Ledge Margin change how far ahead (Combat > Knockdowns)
Parallax: carved walls keep their depth when seen from the side, no swimming (Graphics > Surfaces)
Animated buttons and slipgate panels: no pulsing between frames
Flat screen: WSAD responds at once; corner buttons as an icon row at the top left
VR menus: Spectator camera On/Off switch at the bottom left, with a small live preview above it (size readable?); top-right status box fits its text
New defaults: a fresh config looks and plays like yours (parallax, combat, parry, retro)
Official Campaigns: drawn in the big menu font
Map tips: the CRT tip near the vrstart start appears; REPEAT tips come back; List This Map's Tips works
Map Library: Down the Gutter now plays (page says which map Play starts); reinstall November-Schlacht to get its sky back; AD/Copper maps are flagged
Training dummy: with Dummy Dies on it dies exactly like a grunt (beheaded, head pops by chance, ragdoll, gibs), stands again after 2 s
Music: plays from your own rerelease install in e1m1, hip1m1, r1m1, an mg3 and a dopa map; vrstart hub track is the one you want
Firing range: buttons visible from the far end; doors/buttons don't flicker against frames up close; labels fit their text even after playing a Map Library package
Map Library: Debug > External Map Index shows Download Cache Size (512 MB) and Usage; an oversized cache trims at start

[Menus and maps]
Menus: Menu Detail, VR presets, first-start calibration, Changed Settings (*), Reset This Page and Search all work
Map Library: open it both ways, search, filter, sort, install with progress, Play, uninstall; unsupported packages refused clearly
Map Library: quitting during the first index download doesn't crash; offline start is quiet
Official Campaigns: base Quake hub, tutorial and range work; Dopa plays through (secrets, saves, carried guns, finale)
MG1 developer tests: rune returns, electrode buttons, Horde, revival; quake vibration is comfortable

[Combat]
Melee hits: head, limb and leg multipliers match your shots; hitbox size and head priority feel right
Parry: stops knight and ogre attacks and staggers them
Bullet time: gadget button and wrist tap work; resting or swinging near the wrist doesn't trigger it
Weapons: magazines and blood stay with each gun through holster, throw, pickup and hand-off; map pickups come loaded
Weapons: weights feel as tuned; lightning gun second hotspot; crowbar holster poses
Secrets (e1m1): punching and shooting secret walls gives sparks, not blood; they still open

[Fire and torches]
Burning: torch hits ignite monsters, corpses and crates; flames spread and follow ragdolls; explosive boxes don't burn
Wall torches: grab, punch, shoot off and knock off with a thrown rock
Torch flame: upright, upside down and swung looks right; smoke readable; it can burn your other hand
Map flames (e1m2): braziers and wall torches burn hands and body
Lava nails: nails through a torch flame fizz and set targets on fire

[Gore]
Gibs: squish, stick to walls when thrown, burst when punched but not in two-handed throws
Small gibs and brain chunks: land near the wound; brain chunks fly from head bursts
Blood: on hands, weapons, holstered guns and boxes; health heals only your wounds; water washes
Beheading and head pops: grunt, zombie, fiend, shambler, gremlin, mummy

[Ragdolls and physics]
Ragdolls: every supported monster goes limp unseen (slow motion too), drops its weapon, feels its mass
Ragdolls: grab, force grab, fling, pile up and burn them; wounds stay where they were hit
Corpses: collision modes and stepping over or onto them feel right
Props: two-handed small props never jump; floating props bob, sunk ones rest; big piles behave

[Slipgates]
Slipgates: walk, reach, shoot and throw through; frames are solid; leaning in doesn't teleport you
Slipgates: each visible gate shows its own destination in both eyes, flat and spectator; light and shadows pass through; no splashes
start: the hidden staircase cover stays until all episodes are done

[Graphics]
Retro textures and lighting: crisp blocks with no crawl; per-category settings and Pick overrides work
Quetoo maps: walls by a lamp and the flashlight; pick a specular level

[Sound and recording]
Sound: Full-Band Sound and HRTF Anti-Aliasing place sounds clearly, no hiss
Recording: slow motion with game-time sound and highlight markers line up in Resolve
Spectator camera: no hand ghost, hides head-locked text, Bullet Time effects toggle

[Decide]
Map tips: seen-tips limit (~80), targetname naming clash, tip_delay 0

[Tune]
Weapon damage, grapple, stamina costs, roll weight and throw damage

[Record]
Record new melee takes: the melee test suite can't run without them
