[0. Flat-screen: weapons, movement and look]
Fire the heavy revolver, in VR or on a flat screen: the hammer now visibly cocks all the way back before it falls, with no snap at the start of the fall; the shot comes about 0.11 s after the pull and the rate of fire is the same (Tunables > Heavy Revolver Hammer). {test:flat_revolver_eject}
Kick or drop the crate (not a hand throw) hard into the lit wall torch: it falls off its wall as a thrown crate knocks it; nudged slowly against it, the torch stays. {test:systems_torch_knock}
Flat-screen: lie down and press Space (jump): you stand up as the prone key would, and do not jump, even holding Space; let go and press again to jump; in a low vent Space leaves you lying like the prone key. {test:flatprone}
[1. VR: body, lying down and swimming]
Lie down and crawl with your arms: a hand only drags you when its knuckles are on the floor (a hand a few cm up swims no more; Arm Crawl Floor Height 1, steps of 0.25, 0 allowed) {test:prone_floor}
Crawl hand pose: with Empty Hand Crawls When = Closed (default) an open empty hand resting or sliding on the floor does nothing, a fist (grip or trigger) drags you; Open and Either do as named; the shotgun hand always drags {test:prone_floor}
Crawl any way: pull back to go forward, push ahead to go back, pull sideways to slide sideways {test:prone_floor}
Lying Down Below now goes to 48: try 26-30 (on all fours) and see you lie down without lying flat; getting up still works, and Getting Up Margin (default 2) stops flicker if your head bobs at the limit {test:prone_floor}
Crawl into the 40-high tunnel lying down and get up: only to a crouch; with Lowest Crouched Height 44, getting up inside keeps you lying until you crawl out. {test:prone_tunnel}
Lie down, crawl into the 20-high vent ahead and get up for real inside it: you stay lying and can't move (stick, arm crawl, a step in the room, teleport), the view goes nearly black and blurred and "Lie back down" shows in front of you, the teleporter in the wall above never takes you even with your hands up; lie back down and it all clears and you crawl on. {test:lowceil_vent}
Crouch for real into the 40-high tunnel ahead and stand up inside it: you stay crouched and can't move, the view goes nearly black and blurred and "Crouch back down" shows; crouch again and it clears and you walk on. Is the darkness right, and does it let go at a comfortable height? {test:prone_tunnel}
Flat-screen: with the visor on, centre messages (a locked door's, a secret's, a long tutorial text) pop into a small visor panel just under the top message panel, wrapped to fit, and fade when their time is up; it stays put whether the message panel is shown or hidden, and Visor HUD Look > Centre Messages in Visor off puts them back over the view. {test:flat_visor_center}
Flat-screen: press 3 with the rocket launcher held in both hands: first the left hand lets go of the grip and comes to the empty-hand pose in front of you while the gun stays still, then the right hand passes the gun across to it, then the right hand takes the gun's two-hand grip. {test:flat_hand_swap}
Crouch right next to a corpse and pop one of its limbs with the shotgun without moving: the stump's blood fountain pours from the first moment, as it does from a few steps away; if it still doesn't, take a screenshot right then. {test:flat_blood_pointblank}
With developer 1, shooting things no longer fills the console with "particle2:" lines. {test:flat_blood_pointblank}
Flat-screen: hold the right mouse button with a melee weapon facing a sword grunt: every one of its blows is parried, flat slashes too; turn your back to it and its blows land. {test:flat_parry}
Flat-screen: after a parry your weapon glows gold with embers for the counter's window, as in the headset. {test:flat_parry}
Start the Armour Studio near an armed grunt, then leave the menu (or close the studio's window): the grunt fights again and your blows hurt it. {test:armourstudio_close}

[2. VR: melee and embedded blades]
Hit a grunt with the axe right in front of you: the blood and its red mist come right at the camera, not only further off (your config still has Mist Clear of Eyes at 40: set vr_gore_mist_near 0; Particle Fade Near Eyes and Particles Hidden Nearer at 0 draw it at the eyes too). {test:flat_skewer_gibs}
Skewer a gib or a head on the sword, then snap turn and turn with the stick: it stays on the blade the whole time, with no jump or flicker on the snap. {test:vr_skewer_snap}
Shoot a grunt or a corpse from point blank (flat-screen crouched over it, or the headset with the gun right at it): the blood drops flying close to your eyes are small blocky blood blobs, not big flat red squares; Graphics > Models and Effects > Retro Particle Detail sets how many blocks a drop keeps (0 brings the old flat squares back). {test:flat_blood_pointblank}
Pull a stuck blade along its axis: it slides (harder the deeper) and comes out 1:1, hand and blade gliding back together; an axe only past its depth plus 20 cm with a firm pull (slow: your hand comes off), shaking and buzzing as you near it; hard sideways frees it, across it moves the body and only a fast yank tears it out. {test:embed_stab}
Open your hand: the blade stays in, bleeding; grip it: back on the same axis, no ghost hand; force grab from afar tugs it out to you; the second hand takes the two-handed grip and stays; turning your hand turns a stuck axe a few degrees, a sword less; a sunk handle turns out to be grabbed. {test:embed_stab}
Kill a grunt with the blade held: you keep it, a slow pull carries the corpse (overhead on a sword, dragged by an axe), a firm tug frees it, gibbed it's back in your hand; on a falling body your arm stretches, then lets go. Behead or de-limb with the axe, or cut off a part holding a stuck axe: the axe never floats where it was. {test:embed_stab}
Stab hard: the grunt staggers about 2.5 s and pulling drags it, deep and two-handed it's off balance about 3.5 s; a weak stab: no stagger. The axe swung very hard: about 0.75 s. Ogre: shorter; shambler: a moment, no drag, you slowed; walk away holding a blade: you drag it at a third of your speed, out past arm's reach. {test:embed_stab}
A wound with a blade in it bleeds: sliding the blade makes flesh sounds and spurts every 3 cm, live or dead; held past 10 s, a stream follows the body, stopping when the blade leaves or the body dies. {test:embed_stab}
Kill a grunt messily, stab a gib or its head tip first: it rides the blade (up to 3), the blade heavier; the other hand slides one off or takes it; a slash bursts a loose one; dropped or switched they stay or fall unbroken; a hard gib-first throw or the gib's part hitting a wall bursts it. {test:embed_stab}
An axe swing that cuts off a head or limb keeps it on the axe at the cut (it adds a little weight); a hard stopped swing or a sharp shake flings it off; the other hand pulls it off (about 30 cm); swords don't keep parts (vr_embed_cuton 2). {test:embed_stab}
Throw a sword into a grunt: it bleeds; kill it and the blade rides the corpse; gib it or cut off that limb: the blade falls. Combat > Embedded Blades off: only axes stick, falling out when the body dies. {test:embed_throw}
Stab a grunt deep and hold the sword: swing your hand sideways, pull it back, lift it over your head with one hand: the staggering body (and a dead one) follows your hand closely, a little behind, and hangs on the blade overhead; an axe the same, a bit less; an ogre much harder. Blade Hold Strength (Advanced VR Options > Combat > Embedded Blades, and VR Settings > Gameplay) at x0.25 it lags and sags, at x4 it is glued to the blade. {test:embed_stab}
Skewer a gib on the sword, then turn with the stick (smooth and snap) and walk: the gib stays locked on the blade, no lag or lead behind it. {test:embed_stab}
Stab a crate hard with the sword, or swing the axe hard into a barrel: the blade stays in. Holding it, move aside: the crate drags along, heavily; lift: one hand only tips it, both lift it. Open your hand: it stays in and rides the crate pushed away; grip it again and pull it straight out (harder than out of a body). Break the crate: the blade falls free, back in your hand if held. {test:embed_crates}
Run into a grunt with a held stab: the blade pushes back and you stop in front of it instead of running past (Tunables: Running Stab: Brake, Running Stab: Speed). {test:embed_stab}
Stab a grunt and keep holding while it goes active ragdoll: the grip holds (Blade Hold Strength x1; raise it if it still slips). {test:embed_stab}
Stab a grunt deep with the sword, set Blade Hold Strength to x10 and lift it over your head with one hand, pointing the sword up: the body comes all the way up with the blade, along it too, and swings round with your wrist; at x1 it lags as before. {test:embed_stab}
Get hurt with your hands near your face: your blood streams, splashes and drips still show right at your face (Your Gore: Eye Clearance now 0), and each stream starts on your drawn body or arm, moving with it, not floating off it. {test:playergore}
Cut a limb or head off onto the blade, then punch it or strike it with the other hand's weapon: it bursts with a spray of blood right where it sat on the blade, not just small gibs; if the blood still doesn't show with the blade close to your face, lower Particle Fade Near Eyes and try again. {test:embed_stab}
Swing and chop an axe or sword at a grunt's chest and shoulders: it dies without losing its head; a cut across the neck still beheads it, and the training dummy says "would behead" only for blows it calls a head hit.
Swing the axe hard into a dead grunt's ragdoll: the axe stays in it (grab it back out) instead of the body bursting into gibs.
Chop and slash with the Super Axe's blade: the blows land as the head (and stay in when hard enough), not as pommel strikes; the white blow line with Show Melee Blades ends at the middle of the blade's edge. {test:superaxe_blade}
Stab the training dummy with the sword and chop the axe into a grunt so they stay in, then pull them out: the blade is bloody where it went in, and stays bloody in your hand.
In VR, force grab a brick or a gib: the beam starts from your palm, and the thing stops tumbling as it arrives and sits in the hand upright as it lay.

[3. VR: ragdolls, bodies and armed humanoids]
A grunt hanging from a ledge (vrclimb, shove one off the long ledge) kicks and wiggles its legs and sways a little, and still holds on and pulls up. Tunables: vr_ragdoll_active_hang_kick 2, _hz 1.4, _random 0.6, _sway 1. {test:rag_hang}
Punch or pistol a grunt's ragdoll torso many times: limbs/head may pop, but it never bursts into gibs; a point-blank super shotgun or a rocket still gibs it. vr_corpse_gib_overkill 50, vr_corpse_gib_hit_min 35. {test:rag_gib}
Neck snap standing and on a lifted body: it goes limp, still held. Leg grabs: pull one down by the shins, drag it by the legs, lift it by the legs. Hold one with both hands: no crash. {test:grunts_to_throw}
Knock a grunt off a ledge: it grabs the lip, hangs, pulls up (or drops). Into the pool: it swims to where it can stand; killed in water: it paddles weakly, then goes limp. {test:ragdoll_behaviours}
