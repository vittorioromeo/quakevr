# Quake VR playtest checklist (VR menu > Checklist). One line = one thing to try: what to do and what to look for; tick what works, tell me what doesn't.
# Reload List refreshes this page; no rebuild needed. Ticked items are retired (their marks stay in checklist_ticks.txt); older items are in git history.
# Sections are by feature, in the order to go through them (installer and start-up, flat screen, tutorial and VR interface, weapons and holsters, ropes and chains, armed humanoids, Dwell, Quoth, Arcane Dimensions, Survival Arena, multiplayer and bots, recording and dev tools); empty ones are left out and come back when a line needs them.
# A flat-only line starts "Flat-screen:"; a line that works in either mode starts "Flat-screen or VR:" (try it in either); the rest are for the headset.
# New lines from a ship land in a [New: not yet sorted] section at the top (or in the section a lines file names on its first line, "[section: <name>]"); the next sort moves them into place and drops that section.
# A line ending in {test:<id>} has a Test button: it loads quakevr/scenarios/<id>.txt's scene (docs/vr-port/TESTING.md, "Checklist scenarios"); every line ends in one (at least a minimal scene: the map, where you stand, what is given). Multiplayer lines' scenes start a local game with bots (passive: stand still, never attack, take hits; or active), nothing opened on the network. Flat-screen lines' scenes work under -flatscreenmode too (the game's mode is picked at start-up, not by the scene).
# Lines are only for something new or changed you can see or feel: one line per flow, never "works as before".
