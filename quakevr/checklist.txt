[2. Flat-screen: setup, menus and the Map Library]
Installer: install into a fresh folder; the "Set up the game" page shows the settings tree; pick Snap 45, Left-handed and Medium: set in the game. Change Turning in the game, run Update, change only Turn Speed: Turning stays as you set it.
VR Settings > Gameplay: the big gameplay switches (replacements, drops, melee, knockdowns, holding enemies, bullet time, gore, arena) on one page: check each row's wording.
Flat-screen or VR: open the Map Library a few minutes after the game starts (the first time Slipseer's listing is read slowly, a page every few seconds): Slipseer's maps are listed beside Quaddicted's and the list header counts each; with the bar's Source button on all sources every row shows a Quaddicted or Slipseer tag, and on Slipseer or Quaddicted only that source's maps are listed.
Flat-screen or VR: pick a Slipseer map: within several seconds its detail shows the credit (by its author, from Slipseer), what it needs (Arcane Dimensions, Copper... or only Quake), its description and its screenshot, and the action button changes from "Reading its page..." to Install; say whether the screenshot is a good size and readable.
Flat-screen or VR: in the Map Library, pick a Slipseer map, then another a moment later: each one's description and screenshot appear within a second or two, without waiting for Slipseer's list of maps to finish loading.
Flat-screen or VR: in the Map Library, pick Herzblut or Kadath on Slipseer (hosted elsewhere): their description, screenshot and Install now appear; Install either downloads the zip or says to get it from the map's page.
Flat-screen or VR: install a small vanilla Slipseer map (Drain, for example) and press Play; if the install stops with "it is not a zip" (the site gave a web page), say which map: the site's download may need a choice of file or a sign-in.
Flat-screen or VR: on the installed filter, uninstall the Slipseer map you installed (press twice): it leaves the installed list, and the Map Library still lists it on Slipseer.
Flat-screen or VR: in the Map Library, pick a few Quaddicted maps (ne_ruins, for example; with the index already cached from an earlier session): each one's screenshot shows in the details pane after a moment and changes with the pick; a map with none shows no error text.
Flat-screen or VR: in the Map Library, watch the status line under the buttons while you pick a map and while the list loads: it says what is being fetched (the map's page, its screenshot, a list page, bytes, or a wait for the site's pace), and a failure shows in one line.
Flat-screen or VR: Open the map library (Slipseer or Quaddicted) with the menu tint on and pick a map: its screenshot shows its true colours, not the menu hue.

[7. VR: melee and embedded blades]
Stab a standing monster with the sword (not hard enough to stagger it), keep holding it, and let it walk into a teleporter: the sword comes back to your hand, not held from across the map. {test:embed_tele}
Stab a grunt deep and hold the sword: swing your hand sideways, pull it back, lift it over your head with one hand pointing the sword up: the staggering body (and a dead one) follows your hand closely, a little behind, and hangs on the blade overhead; an axe the same, a bit less; an ogre much harder. Blade Hold Strength (Advanced VR Options > Combat > Embedded Blades, and VR Settings > Gameplay): at x0.25 it lags and sags, at x10 the body comes all the way up the blade and swings round with your wrist. {test:embed_stab}
Hold a sword left in a dead grunt (or a staggering one) by its handle and lift your hand a little, turning your wrist as you do: the sword and the drawn hand stay on your real hand and rise with it, no further (they used to drift 30 cm off and jump up with the wrist); the body comes with the blade and goes round with your wrist. Grip the handle again a little off it: the handle comes to your hand in a fifth of a second. {test:embed_handle}
Grip the handle of a sword left in a crate or in a body, the blade rolled or tilted any way: the drawn hand lies on the handle exactly as it holds that sword normally, not turned on it. {test:embed_handle}
Stab a standing grunt hard so that it staggers and keep hold: if your hand went on past the hilt, or stopped short of it, the body is pushed or pulled along the blade until the handle is in your hand. Say if that feels wrong after a stab (Debug > Tunables > Moving: Held by the Handle turns it off, Moving: Handle Comes to the Hand sets how fast). {test:embed_handle}
Stab a wooden crate and keep hold of the sword: move your hand aside and the crate comes with it, the handle staying in your real hand; lift, and one hand only tips it; turning your wrist doesn't swing the crate about. It is held by a joint at the handle now (Debug > Tunables > Moving: Held by the Handle turns it off). {test:embed_crates}
Leave a blade in a crate or a barrel and push or pull the crate with your other hand or your body: the blade goes with it, where it went in. Grip the handle again a little to the side of it: the handle comes to your hand across the blade. {test:embed_crates}
With a gib, a head or a limb skewered on your sword, reach for it with your free hand: the grip takes it only with your hand at the gib itself, and the hand is drawn on the gib's surface (it used to float 9 cm or more off it, on a ball the size of its box). {test:skewer_grip}
Run at a grunt and throw the knight's sword or the dagger hard, point first: it dies whole (no gibs), falling with the blade deep in its body (about 80% of the blade in); with Quad it still gibs, and handle-first or flat throws are as before. {test:throw_nogib}
Hold the dagger or a sword and press B (or Y on the off hand): it turns half round in your hand over a fifth of a second with a click, blade down, the thumb over the pommel; press again to flip it back. {test:revgrip}
Toss the dagger, let it turn over in the air and catch it blade down: you hold it reversed; caught blade up, upright. A force grab always takes it upright, and a reversed blade holstered and drawn again stays reversed. {test:revgrip}
Held blade down, stab the grunt from above: the blade sticks in, and your hand on its handle in the body looks the same as holding it. {test:revgrip}
