Own DOOM? Drop its WAD files here.

Put DOOM.WAD (The Ultimate DOOM), DOOM1.WAD (the shareware episode), DOOM2.WAD, TNT.WAD or PLUTONIA.WAD in this
folder (any letter case). Quake VR's wrist gadget then plays it instead of Freedoom: a known release is recognised by
its checksum and named in VR Settings > Wrist Gadget > Doom, where you can also pick another one.

Installed on Steam (The Ultimate DOOM, DOOM II, Final DOOM, DOOM + DOOM II) or GOG, it is found by itself: nothing to
copy. Without any, Freedoom (free, bundled in quakevr/doom) is played.
