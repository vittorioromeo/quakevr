Violacea
You have traveled far. Awaken the dormant city and find what lies below​

A conversion of my Q30_deathmatch jam map to singleplayer.

  • Requires Arcane Dimensions 1.8.
  • Tested on Quakespasm and VKQuake, playtesters also had no issues on Ironwail.
  • Supports co-op (untested but should work fine) and the original deathmatch it was meant for.
  • 10-15 minutes long with a small-mediumish monster count.






Bug reports/feedback welcome. Do not point out misaligned textures or I will cry and it will be your fault.

I hope you enjoy it!
