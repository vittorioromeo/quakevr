ericw-tools' light, shipped with Quake VR for the in-game relighting
====================================================================

The files in this folder (light.exe, embree4.dll, tbb12.dll, tbbmalloc.dll) are ericw-tools 2.0.0-alpha11's
light and the libraries it needs, unchanged, from its Windows release:

    https://github.com/ericwa/ericw-tools/releases/tag/2.0.0-alpha11
    (ericw-tools-2.0.0-alpha11-win64.zip)

Quake VR runs light.exe as a separate program (VR Settings > Advanced VR Options > Graphics > Relighting), giving it
a copy of a map and reading the files it writes. It is not linked into Quake VR, and Quake VR does not depend on it
to run: delete this folder and the game works as before (only the in-game relighting needs light, and it can be
pointed at another copy with vr_relight_tool).
