## Data and assets

- Quake's own models, sounds, textures and maps belong to id Software. The player's copies are used; `relight_maps.py`
  re-lights them locally and nothing derived from them is distributed.
- **Generated by this port's scripts** (`Misc/quakevr/`): the body (`make_vrbody.py`), the hand taper
  (`taper_hand.py`), the bloody hand skins (`make_bloody_hands.py`). All colours are taken from Quake's palette and
  its own models (for example, the armour colours come from `progs/armor.mdl`).

## Techniques, research and references

### Body IK
- Parger, Mueller, Schmalstieg, Steinberger, [Human upper-body inverse kinematics for increased embodiment in
  consumer-grade VR](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3281505.3281529), VRST 2018. There is also an
  [author's post](https://dabeschte.github.io/paper/2018/12/01/vrarmik.html) and [VRArmIK](https://github.com/dabeschte/VRArmIK)
  (MIT).

### Throwing and grabbing
- Valve: [SteamVR Unity plugin](https://github.com/ValveSoftware/steamvr_unity_plugin) (`Throwable.cs`,
  `SteamVR_Behaviour_Pose.cs`), and the Half-Life: Alyx developer commentary.
- Unity XR Interaction Toolkit (`XRGrabInteractable`).
- Meta Interaction SDK (`StandardVelocityCalculator`, `RANSACVelocity`, physics guidance).
- Godot XR Tools (`function_pickup.gd`, `velocity_averager.gd`).
- HurricaneVR and Auto Hand documentation.
- Articles:
  - [Why throwing in VR sucks](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/why-throwing-in-vr-sucks--and-how-to-make-it-better);
  - NormalVR, [Throwing throw-down](https://www.normalvr.com/blog/throwing-throw-down/);
  - Wilco Schoneveld, [Challenges of a WebVR throwing game](https://dev.to/wilcoschoneveld/challenges-of-a-web-vr-throwing-game-1c66).
- Papers: arXiv [2208.02166](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2208.02166), CEUR-WS [Vol-2470 p33](https://ceur-ws.org/Vol-2470/p33.pdf).
- Rec Room, patent US10990169B2 (aim assist), for reference only.

### Graphics (GRAPHICS.md, LIGHTING.md)
- **DarkPlaces** (LordHavoc and contributors):
  - the shadow-map face-size LOD `radius × precision / √(distance / radius)`;
  - halving and repacking when the atlas is full;
  - guard bands, side culling, and the idea of excluding a light's own entity.
- **FTE QuakeWorld** (Spike and the FTE team): per-light cached world shadow meshes, and presets.
- **Quetoo** (Jay Dolan): shadow tiles redrawn only when a hash of the light and casters changes (planned).
- **q2pro-ng** (skullernet): per-pixel dynamic lights shared by world and models, a D32F shadow atlas with a static
  world-only cache under the moving casters, which is the same split used here.
- **RBDOOM-3-BFG** (Robert Beckebans): shadow-atlas LOD and presets. **The Dark Mod:** soft-shadow quality settings.
- **vkQuake** (Axel Gneiting "Novum" and contributors): GPU lightmap dynamic lights and ray-query shadows (`r_rtshadows`);
  **Ironwail** issues #16, #191, #262, #329 (the author's notes on shadows and per-pixel dynamic lights).
- **Hexenwail** (issues #78, #97), **yquake2** (GL3/GL4 per-pixel dynamic lights, normal nudge), and the **Quake / Quake II re-releases** (id Software, Nightdive;
  released QuakeC and game code: `dynamiclight` and `shadowlight*`).
- **ericw-tools documentation:** `-dirt`, `-bounce`, `-extra4`, `-lit`, BSPX.
- **Xonotic** effect presets.
- **Alex Vlachos (Valve):** "Advanced VR Rendering", GDC 2015, and "Advanced VR Rendering Performance",
  GDC 2016. Also [the_lab_renderer](https://github.com/ValveSoftware/the_lab_renderer).
- **id Tech 6/7:** Tiago Sousa and Jean Geffroy, "The devil is in the details", SIGGRAPH 2016;
  [Adrian Courrèges, Doom (2016) graphics study](https://www.adriancourreges.com/blog/2016/09/09/doom-2016-graphics-study/);
  [Simon Coenen, Doom Eternal graphics study](https://simoncoenen.com/blog/programming/graphics/DoomEternalStudy).
- **Matt Pettineo (MJP):** [A Sampling of Shadow Techniques](https://therealmjp.github.io/posts/shadow-maps/).
- **Ola Olsson, Markus Billeter, Ulf Assarsson:** Clustered Deferred and Forward Shading, HPG 2012. Also Olsson
  et al., More Efficient Virtual Shadow Maps for Many Lights.
- **Emil Persson:** Practical Clustered Shading.
- **Holbert:** normal-offset shadows, GDC 2011.
- **Meta:** display guidance, and the Oculus Unreal renderer notes (MSAA over TAA; forward shading).
- **Unity:** HDRP cached shadows, URP shadow atlas.
- **NVIDIA:** VRSS, and the shading-rate-image and multiview samples.
- **Ilias Kapouranis:** Is single-pass stereo worth it?
- **UploadVR:** Doom 3 Quest performance notes.
- **Virtual Desktop / VDXR:** [mbucchia's VirtualDesktop-OpenXR](https://github.com/mbucchia/VirtualDesktop-OpenXR).
- **Khronos:** the OpenXR specification.
