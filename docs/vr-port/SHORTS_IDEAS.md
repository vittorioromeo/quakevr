
**Combat and melee**
- **Disarm and use it:** knock a grunt's gun away and shoot him with it, or catch his thrown weapon.
- **Directional parry and counter:** block a knight's sword, then counter.
- **Embedded blades:** stab a grunt and leave the sword in the body; lift the ragdoll by the blade; pull it out with force grab.
- **Beheading and limb cutting:** an axe through the neck, the head sitting in the blade; gibs sliding on the edge.
- **Shield or melee duel** against an armed grunt that parries back.
- **Throwing anything:** crates, torches, heads and gibs as weapons that knock enemies into ragdolls.

**Physics and emergence** (your strongest pitch)
- **The chain reaction:** a rocket kills a grunt, the body flies into a wall torch, the torch falls on a crate, and the fire spreads. It was built and tested this week.
- **Active ragdolls:** shove or tackle grunts, watch them stumble, get up, and fight on.
- **Fire spreading:** burning grunts setting crates and each other alight, and water putting them out.
- **Lightning in water:** zapping a flooded room, and a Shambler's bolt now conducting too.
- **Dropping things on enemies** from a ledge: a crate on a grunt's head.
